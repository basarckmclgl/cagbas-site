Personal Data Protection and Cookie Policy

This notice explains contact, newsletter and analytics-cookie processing on cagbas.com.

1. Data controller

Data controller: Başar Çekmecelioğlu (natural person). Contact and data-subject requests: cagbas.2026@gmail.com. A private home address is not published. This is the contact channel stated here; statutory identity and application requirements still apply.

2. Data and purposes

Contact form

Name, email address, required phone number, subject and message are processed to receive, assess and answer enquiries, handle relevant pre-contractual communications and establish, exercise or defend legal claims where necessary. Form entries are stored in the website's Cloudflare D1 database; notification emails are sent using Gmail. Failure of an email notification does not necessarily mean the database submission was lost.

Newsletter

Your email address and subscription/permission records are used to verify your subscription request and send the newsletter. A separate marketing-consent checkbox is required. Verification emails are sent through Brevo; the verification link is valid for 24 hours. Once an unsubscribe function is used, further newsletter delivery is stopped. Submitting the contact form does not subscribe you to the newsletter.

Cookies and analytics

Essential technologies may be used for core site functions and security. Google Analytics is not loaded until you actively consent to analytics cookies. If you reject them, the analytics tag is not run. Your preference is stored in your browser's local storage and can be changed later through the cookie-preference interface.

3. Legal grounds and collection

Data is collected electronically through website forms, email correspondence and cookie/local-storage technologies. Depending on the nature of an enquiry, contact data may be processed where necessary for pre-contractual steps at your request, the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims, or another applicable legal ground. Newsletter delivery relies on your separate, withdrawable marketing consent. Non-essential analytics cookies remain disabled unless you affirmatively consent.

4. Service providers and recipients

For the limited purposes described above, data may be processed by Cloudflare for hosting, security and database services; Google Gmail for contact notifications; Brevo for newsletter verification/delivery; and Google Analytics for visit statistics only after consent. These providers' affiliates, infrastructure partners and processing locations may depend on account and contractual settings. If personal data is transferred abroad, an appropriate mechanism under Article 9 of Turkish Law No. 6698 and any required notifications must be in place. Provider account regions, contracts and transfer settings must be verified before publication.

5. Retention and deletion

Contact records are retained for the time needed to respond to and manage the enquiry, and thereafter only for applicable legal obligations or limitation periods needed to handle potential claims. Newsletter addresses remain while the subscription is active and are removed from sending lists when it ends. Consent and unsubscribe evidence may be retained in a limited form where necessary to demonstrate the permission history and meet legal obligations. Data whose purpose or retention requirement has ended is deleted, destroyed or anonymised in accordance with applicable rules. Exact periods must be set in the data inventory based on actual operations and statutory requirements.

6. Your rights and requests

Under Article 11 of Law No. 6698, you may have the right to learn whether your data is processed, request information, learn the purposes and recipients, request correction or deletion where applicable, request notification of such actions to recipients, object to certain automated decisions and seek compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

Send your request to cagbas.2026@gmail.com with the subject “KVKK Data Subject Request”. Include sufficient information to verify your identity and assess the request. The controller responds as soon as possible and no later than 30 days, subject to statutory procedure and identity-verification requirements.

Analytics cookies are enabled only after your affirmative choice. Selecting “Reject” prevents the analytics tag from running. Google Analytics and browser cookie names/periods may vary with configuration and service updates; the live cookie inventory must be checked before publication.
